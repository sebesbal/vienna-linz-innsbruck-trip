What to do and see

This plan leaves a full day for the mountains and makes room for Innsbruck’s museums and Old Town. Attraction admission is additional to the base trip estimate.

Friday 2 October · arrive and explore the Old Town

  1. 13:45–15:00Arrive at Innsbruck Hbf, travel to your hotel and leave luggage.
  2. 15:00–17:00Walk along Maria-Theresien-Straße into the Old Town. See the Golden Roof; if you want a museum visit, the Golden Roof Museum is open until 17:00.
  3. 17:00–eveningCross to the Inn river for the colourful house view, then have dinner in the Old Town.

Saturday 3 October · mountain panorama and imperial history

  1. 09:00–12:30Ride the Nordkette cable cars from the Old Town to Seegrube and Hafelekar. The adult round trip is €56; check weather and operating status before setting out.
  2. 12:30–14:00Lunch back in the city and time to rest.
  3. 14:00–16:00Visit the Hofkirche and see the bronze figures around Emperor Maximilian’s monument (Saturday 09:00–17:00; €9 adult).
  4. EveningDinner in the centre. Optional: the Hofkirche also lists a special Long Night of Museums opening from 18:00 to midnight; it has separate ticketing.

Sunday 4 October · Ambras Castle and return

  1. 10:00–13:00Visit Ambras Castle and its collections (daily 10:00–17:00; €16 online adult ticket). Bus 540 connects it with Innsbruck Hbf.
  2. 13:00–16:30Return to the centre for lunch, a café stop and any Old Town sights you missed.
  3. 16:30–18:00Collect luggage and reach Innsbruck Hbf with a comfortable margin.
  4. 18:16–22:52Direct WESTbahn train to Wien Westbahnhof.

For Nordkette, Hofkirche and Ambras together, consider the 48-hour Innsbruck Card (€79 per adult), activated Saturday morning. It includes those attractions and local public transport. Check the current inclusion terms before buying.
